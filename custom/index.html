Custom

We collaborate with residential and commercial clients on custom fine furniture and built-in millwork, mostly along West Coast USA and Canada.

Collaborations are some of our favourite projects and give us an opportunity to design for your particular space.

Project Feature
.World

Collaboration with Sam Altman’s World project (world.org) on bent wood community tables and seating.

Designed to bring people together at global flagships in San Francisco, Munich + Barcelona.

“Working with Studio Fritz on our custom furniture felt like a natural fit. Lucas brought exceptional craft and care to every stage, from design and material selection to the final build. Whether refining his wood-bending technique or introducing stainless-steel details, he made each piece feel distinctive and full of savoir-faire.

His designs balance mid-century influences with a contemporary sensibility. Lucas’ deep understanding of wood, materials, and process, combined with his creativity and willingness to experiment, made all the difference. The result is a beautiful collection that people are drawn to gather around every day.”

– Constanzia Delort-Laval, Client Architect

Project Feature
.Interior Architects

Large, 200 lb walnut table with dove joints designed for architects and interior designers to spread out concepts for clients.

Made in collaboration with global architecture firm Interior Architects (interiorarchitects.com).

Located in historic Firestone Tire Building, 400 Westlake in downtown Seattle.

“Working with Studio Fritz reminded me why I got into this industry in the first place—to be part of creating spaces filled with the work of talented artists and craftsmen.

The quality, craftsmanship, and story behind the custom furniture they created for our space elevated the entire design and gave it a sense of character that simply couldn’t be achieved any other way.”

– Abigail Metcalfe, Client Interior Designer
